## 🗓️ Detailed Breakdown

### Phase 1 — Registration & Track Selection
**Aug 26 → Sep 2, 2026**

- [ ] Form your team (max 5 members)
- [ ] Choose 1 track + 1 problem statement (out of 10)
- [ ] Submit via official registration form:
  - Team name, track, and problem statement
  - Short statement of why you chose this problem
  - Brief description of intended approach
  - CV of each team member

**🔴 Deadline: September 2, 2026**

---

### Phase 2 — Research & Initial Development
**Sep 2 → Sep 26, 2026**

- [ ] Review state of the art for your problem
- [ ] Define & justify your technical approach
- [ ] Set up simulation/experimental environment
- [ ] Begin implementation, get early results

**Mandatory Deliverables (due end of phase):**
1. **Pitch Video** — max 2 minutes, explains problem + core idea + approach
2. **Interim Research Paper** (IEEE double-column format recommended):
   - Title & Authors (project title, track, team members)
   - Abstract & Keywords (150–250 words, 3–5 keywords)
   - I. Introduction
   - II. Related Work
   - III. Methodology
   - IV. Experiments & Roadmap
   - V. Conclusion
   - References

🏆 *Best Scientific Paper award given at the congress.*

**🔴 Deadline: September 26, 2026**

---

### Mid-Review — Evaluation & Pre-Selection
**Sep 26 → Sep 30, 2026**

- Elimination round — only teams scoring **≥ 60/100** continue to Phase 3
- Feedback from jury should be incorporated into Phase 3 work

**🔴 Result window closes: September 30, 2026**

---

### Phase 3 — Final Development
**Oct 1 → Oct 14, 2026**

- [ ] Execute plan defended at mid-way evaluation
- [ ] Incorporate jury feedback
- [ ] Build working prototype/model/demonstrator

**Deliverables:**
- Final technical report
- Repository with documented source code / model / simulation environment
- Results & evaluation against your problem's performance indicators

**🔴 Deadline: October 14, 2026**

---

### Phase 4 — Congress: Final Pitch & Poster Session
**D-Day (dates TBA by organizers)**

- **Final Pitch:** Finalist teams pitch live before the expert jury + interactive Q&A
- **Poster Session (open to all teams, finalist or not):** Present a scientific poster
  - Templates & guidelines shared shortly after registration

## 📊 Scoring Grid (out of 100)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Understanding of the problem | 15 |
| Relevance & originality of solution | 15 |
| Technical architecture & design | 20 |
| Feasibility & realism | 15 |
| Actual prototype progress | 20 |
| Methodology & planning | 10 |
| Presentation & command of project | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Passing threshold (mid-review): 60/100**

**Bonus:** +1 pt for having at least one IEEE IAS YP member on the team.
